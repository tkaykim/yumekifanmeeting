## 1. Introduction

The Yumeki Fan Meeting Goods Sales Website is an e-commerce platform designed for fans to purchase exclusive merchandise related to the Yumeki fan meeting. This platform aims to provide a seamless and efficient shopping experience, allowing fans to easily acquire goods and enhance their engagement with the event.

## 2. Goals

*   Enable fans to purchase Yumeki fan meeting goods conveniently.
*   Reduce long wait times for goods purchases at the fan meeting venue.
*   Provide a platform for fans to acquire limited edition merchandise.
*   Enhance the overall fan experience by facilitating easy access to goods.

## 3. Target Audience

*   Primary target audience: Yumeki fans
*   Demographics: Predominantly individuals aged 20-30.

## 4. Features

### 4.1 Core Features

*   **Product Catalog & Details:**
    *   Display a comprehensive list of available goods with high-quality images and detailed descriptions.
    *   Include options for different sizes (S, M, L for T-shirts; M, L for hoodies) and variations (Love it, MissMe for acrylic keychains).
*   **Shopping Cart & Order Management:**
    *   Allow users to add items to a shopping cart.
    *   Enable users to modify quantities or remove items from the cart.
    *   Provide a clear and intuitive checkout process.
*   **Payment Processing:**
    *   Integrate secure payment gateway options (credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
    *   Ensure transaction security and data protection.
*   **User Account Management:**
    *   Enable users to create and manage accounts.
    *   Allow users to save addresses and payment information for faster checkout.
*   **Shipping & Pickup Options:**
    *   Offer options for fan meeting venue pickup and standard shipping.
    *   Provide clear instructions and timelines for each option.
*   **Order History:**
    *   Allow users to view their past orders and track shipping status.

### 4.2 Additional Features

*   **Real-Time Inventory Display:**
    *   Show real-time stock levels for each product.
    *   Indicate when items are low in stock or sold out.
*   **Social Sharing:**
    *   Enable users to share product details on social media platforms.

## 5. Use Cases

*   **Convenient Goods Purchase:**
    *   Fans can easily browse and purchase goods from the comfort of their homes.
*   **Limited Edition Access:**
    *   The platform offers exclusive access to limited edition merchandise.

## 6. Pain Points

*   **Long Wait Times:**
    *   The platform addresses the issue of long wait times at the fan meeting venue by allowing fans to pre-order goods.

## 7. User Goals

*   **Easy & Fast Purchase:**
    *   Users aim to quickly and easily purchase desired goods.
*   **Event Preparation:**
    *   Fans want to secure goods in advance to enhance their fan meeting experience.
*   **Exclusive Ownership:**
    *   Users seek the joy of owning limited edition merchandise.

## 8. Release Criteria

*   All core features must be fully functional and tested.
*   The platform should be responsive and accessible on various devices.
*   Security measures for payment processing and user data must be implemented.

## 9. Future Considerations

*   Integration with fan community platforms.
*   Loyalty programs and rewards for frequent buyers.
*   Expansion of merchandise offerings based on fan feedback.

## 10. KPIs

*   Website traffic and user engagement.
*   Conversion rates and sales revenue.
*   Customer satisfaction scores.
*   Social media engagement and shares.

## 11. Glossary

*   **Fan Meeting:** An event where Yumeki interacts with her fans.
*   **Goods:** Merchandise available for purchase related to Yumeki and the fan meeting.
*   **Limited Edition:** Exclusive items available in limited quantities.
